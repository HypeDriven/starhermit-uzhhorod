# Uzhhorod: After the Rain

Replace the former miniature-city simulation with a complete, visually coherent river adventure on StarHermit Engine 1.38.0.

The player pilots a mail boat through three painted river scenes, retrieves nine floating letters, delivers them to matching sealed landings, and chooses a short reply to each. Each completed chapter advances afternoon to blue hour to nightfall. The final delivery closes the story.

Use FLUX scenery, a TRELLIS boat, an original YuE2 score, and generated river/paper sound effects. Keep UI sparse, typography legible, and touch/keyboard navigation equivalent. Provide gentle current, pause, letter reading, local saves, and a confirmed restart. Retired-game saves use a separate key and must remain untouched.

Deliver editable source, reproducible asset-generation scripts and provenance, meaningful gameplay checks, and a rebuilt web package. Publication is a separate action.
