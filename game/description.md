# Uzhhorod: After the Rain

The rain has stopped. Nine letters have slipped from a bicycle basket into the Uzh. There is still time to bring them home.

Pilot a little wooden mail boat through a painted riverside, from the old quay in late afternoon to the willows at blue hour and the footbridge at nightfall. Recover pale envelopes from the current, follow their seals to the right landing, and leave a small reply. A book returned after twenty years. A cutting brought home to the garden. A window left lit across the water.

Three chapters. Nine letters. No timer, no lost lives, and no hurry.

Click or tap the water to steer, or use WASD and the arrow keys. Tap a matching landing to deliver, or press E nearby. Read your collected letters at any time. Gentle current makes sailing easier; progress saves automatically on your device.

Original painted scenery, a textured 3D boat, and a gentle instrumental score accompany an imagined evening in Uzhhorod. Characters, letters, and events are fictional; the scenery is an artistic interpretation.
