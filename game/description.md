Explore a living miniature of Uzhhorod, Ukraine, in a calm single-player adventure about memory, history and everyday acts of care.

Restore four landmark memories through light-ring, musical, water-channel and paper-boat puzzles. Follow historical clues, find six small stories, help three residents and complete an evolving letter. Study the city's architecture, connect evidence in the city archive, craft and test floating river lights, host evening walks and curate your own riverside exhibition.

A changing town day brings markets, reading circles, recitals, rain and mist. Optional guides and assistance settings let you explore at your own pace. Play with keyboard, mouse, touch or gamepad; progress saves locally.

The town is a stylized miniature. Historical summaries link to museum and municipal sources; the residents, restoration mechanisms and river-light gathering are game fiction.
